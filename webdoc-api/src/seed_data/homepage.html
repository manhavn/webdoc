How journal publishing has evolved

From manual email workflows to a connected, standards-aware publishing platform.

Stage 1

Manual

Traditional — hard to standardise
Submissions arrive by email or forms, with no shared tracking or consistent metadata.

  • Documents get lost and progress is hard to track
  • Manual work consumes editorial time
  • No central archive or audit trail
  • Difficult to meet national and international standards
  • Limited reach to authors and readers
Stage 2

Legacy OJS

A low-cost starting point
Basic submission and publishing, but many workflows remain fragmented or hard to configure.

  • Covers basic editorial standards
  • Digitises submission and review
  • Limited compliance tooling and integrations
  • Advanced workflows require custom work
  • Interface can be complex for non-technical users
